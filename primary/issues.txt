ISSUES TO POSSIBLY REVISIT
============================================================

1. exposure_pooled / exposure_yr count all ICE case activity, not just SC detainers
------------------------------------------------------------
Where: main.r, county_exposure_pooled and county_exposure_yr (built from sc_clean)

What's happening:
- Both variables are cases / population * 10000, where "cases" = every row in
  sc_clean (secure1904.csv) for a county, regardless of whether that row has a
  detainer_date.
- Only some rows are genuine SC detainers. The rest come through other pathways
  (CAP, 287(g), border patrol, non-custodial arrest, etc.) that have nothing to
  do with Secure Communities specifically - same issue we fixed for the
  treated/first_detainer_year variable earlier, just not yet applied here.
- So exposure_pooled/exposure_yr currently measure "rate of any ICE case
  activity" per county, not "rate of SC detainers" per county.

Concrete numbers (checked 2026-07):
- 2,723 counties have at least one case row of any kind (current exposure_pooled
  universe).
- Only 1,746 of those counties have at least one row with a non-missing
  detainer_date (true SC detainer).
- Restricting to detainer-only rows would drop 977 counties (about 36% of the
  current universe) to zero/NA exposure.
- Among the 1,746 counties that do have at least one detainer, detainer rows are
  typically a small share of all case rows for that county (median 15%, mean
  19%) - so a detainer-only measure would also run roughly 5x lower almost
  everywhere it's still nonzero.

Status: RESOLVED (07/02/2026), revised same day - see below.

First pass (07/02/2026, superseded): restricted exposure_pooled/exposure_yr to
!is.na(detainer_date) only. Ran cleanly but dropped exposure_pooled NA share in
main from 41% to 75% (1,131 of 1,509 rows) - too strict, since SC is a passive
fingerprint-screening mechanism at booking, not a discretionary referral. A case
can be legitimately SC-attributable even when the match never rose to a formal
detainer, so requiring detainer_date specifically excluded a lot of real SC
exposure.

Revised approach (07/02/2026, current): checked latest_apprehension_method x
has-detainer_date. CAP Local Incarceration is 240,868 rows total, only 33% with
a detainer_date - this is exactly the local-jail-booking pathway SC's fingerprint
interoperability applies to, so it counts as SC-attributable regardless of
detainer_date. CAP Federal/State Incarceration and 287(g) Program are excluded
even though also booking-based, since those are screened independent of county-
level SC activation (federal/state facility screening) or are a legally distinct
partnership program (287(g)), respectively. Border patrol/non-custodial arrest/
located/inspections/other are excluded since none involve local jail booking.

New filter: !is.na(detainer_date) | apprehension_method == "CAP Local Incarceration"
Result: exposure_pooled NA share in main improved to 58% (882 of 1,509 rows),
better coverage than the detainer-only version while still excluding border/
287(g)/federal-state-incarceration activity that isn't gated by county SC status.

Still unresolved: for CAP Local Incarceration rows without a detainer_date
(~161k), there's no clean encounter/booking date in the data - only departed_date,
which lags the true encounter (same issue flagged elsewhere in this file). Kept
the existing coalesce(detainer_date, departed_date) year assignment for
exposure_yr as an imprecise-but-informative fallback. early_activator/
late_activator are unaffected by any of this - they're built entirely from the
ICE activation roster (sc_activation_dates.csv), not from sc_clean case dates.


2. exposure_yr has no data for the 2017 ag census wave
------------------------------------------------------------
Where: main.r, county_exposure_yr joined into main on (state, county, year)

What's happening:
- exposure_yr is now merged into main (07/02/2026) so heterogeneous-exposure-
  intensity regressions can vary exposure by census wave instead of relying only
  on the single all-years-pooled exposure_pooled figure.
- Checked qualifying (SC-attributable) rows in sc_clean by year: they run through
  2015 and then drop to zero. This matches the ICE source document's own coverage
  window ("Monthly Statistics through February 28, 2015") - the file has other,
  unrelated records with departed_dates stretching to 2019, but none of those
  qualify as SC-attributable under the current filter, so none of them carry a
  year past 2015 under this definition.

Concrete numbers (checked 07/02/2026), main filtered to the three ag census
years (503 counties each):
- year 2007: 503/503 NA on exposure_yr (correct - pre-SC baseline, expected).
- year 2012: 374/503 NA, 129 counties with real signal, mean 4.49, max 72.1.
- year 2017: 503/503 NA (data ceiling, not a join bug - no qualifying case data
  exists past 2015).

Practical implication: any regression using exposure_yr only has identifying
variation in the 2012 cross-section. A heterogeneous-exposure-intensity test that
also wants to speak to the 2017 wave will need exposure_pooled (or something
built from it) instead of exposure_yr for that year, since exposure_yr simply
carries no information there.

Status: OPEN - not treating this as broken (exposure_yr is joined and working
correctly), just flagging that its usefulness is effectively limited to the 2012
wave given the data's coverage window. Revisit if the 2017 comparison turns out
to matter for the exposure-intensity models specifically.
